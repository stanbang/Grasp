# Recorded ingestion showcase

Mode: Experience. An interviewer can replay the work that turns a note into a map, then inspect a source or ask about it. Success is finding the passage behind an answer and knowing which parts were recorded offline.

## Direction contract

**Thesis.** The source page unfolds into a constellation. Its text stays traceable after the page leaves the canvas.

**Visual world.** Keep Grasp's navy star chart, source-reader borders and teal signal. Use the existing system type stack. Colour and links carry the state of the graph.

**Story.** Choose a public sample. Watch its pages become chunks and links. Inspect a star, ask a question and open the citation to check the answer.

**First viewport.** A spare full-bleed star field sits behind the centered headline and sample strip. The composer anchors the lower center. History opens at the edge. During playback a compact inlay puts page text and stage controls in reach without covering the whole canvas.

**Form.** `seed: none (pinned)` by the brief's centered composer, cinematic replay and dark star-chart world. The owner authorized self-review. The reference chat informed message placement, prompt suggestions and collapsible history. Use Grasp's own typography, colors, icons and wording.

**Finish.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Keep `/` available for real local study. The public route accepts only bundled samples and never reads dropped files. Use the mock pipeline to record real stages and cite exact passages. Playback includes pause, skip, speed and restart. Reduced motion uses manual steps, and keyboard and touch users can open samples, concepts and citations. Provide a 2D graph when WebGL is unavailable or the visitor selects it.

## Teaching walkthrough

The reader can open the walkthrough from the landing page or return to it after loading a sample. Begin with one question from the networks note. Show the note-preparation lane and the question-answering lane as separate sequences. The same quote should appear in the worked example, claim check and source dialog.

Give the five interview questions their own topics. Use controls where a reader can see the consequence of a choice, such as changing an archived retrieval setting or checking a claim against a quote. Keep the replay available behind the walkthrough and pause its clock while the reader is away. At phone widths put topic selection above the reading panel.
