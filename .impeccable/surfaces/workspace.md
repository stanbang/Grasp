# Study workspace

MODE: Operate.

THESIS: Keep a student's canvas open while the source and its contracts remain one click away.

OWN-WORLD: Dark navy and blue-grey surfaces, readable cool white type, one pale teal action colour. Gold is reserved for mastered nodes in Phase 3 onward.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Small top bar, source rail on the left, large quiet canvas, optional source reader on the right. The empty state offers the sample note and data contracts. Health appears at the bottom of the rail without a grid of metric cards.

FORM: The user's pinned star-chart direction takes precedence over seed:02c41993e524441f. The celestial-atlas challenger supports the brief. The gridded specimen's discipline informs alignment; airport wayfinding informs short action labels. Their visual costumes, decorative yellow and catalogue grids are declined because they obscure the study workspace. The generated direction roll was used as a critique input, not as permission to replace the user's pinned direction.

SIGNATURE: A sparse static star field gives the empty canvas depth. Real concept nodes, force motion and camera controls wait until Phase 3. Clicking the sample reveals the exact original note; the canvas remains the spatial anchor.

SCOPE: Phase 1 only. Sample reader, live schema inspector, health and error recovery. No document ingestion, interactive graph, chat or quiz implementation.

STATES: API connecting, ready, unavailable with retry; sample loading and error; schema selection; source-panel open and closed; narrow screens; reduced motion.

FINISH: Check the running desktop and mobile preview, use Impeccable critique and polish, and record limitations. The preview must not depend on a remote font, model, image or 3D renderer.
